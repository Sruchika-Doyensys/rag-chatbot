Acme Corp - Employee Handbook (sample)

Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

Leave Policy
Employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per year, plus 12 casual/sick leave days. Unused annual leave up to 10 days can be carried forward to the next year. Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 3 days in advance.

Reimbursements
Travel and meal expenses for client visits are reimbursed within 15 days of submitting receipts through the Finance portal. The daily meal allowance is INR 1,000.

IT Support
For laptop or access issues, email it-support@acme.example or call extension 4040. Support is available 9 AM to 9 PM on weekdays.
